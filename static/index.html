Consented Profile & Data Rights

verified_user MoSJE PM-AJAY Data Protection & Consent Notice

Your profile is stored strictly for deterministic eligibility matching, skill roadmap generation, and GIA benefit tracking. We never share your data with unauthorized third parties. You have the right to view, modify, or permanently erase your record at any time.

privacy_tip Data Minimisation & Purpose Specification

In accordance with data-minimisation principles, Nivara stores only the minimal fields essential for deterministic livelihood assistance:

  • Caste Category (SC/ST/OBC) [Encrypted at rest]: Required strictly to verify statutory reservation eligibility under PM-AJAY GIA and DSFDC welfare mandates.
  • Income Bracket / Annual Income [Encrypted at rest]: Evaluated strictly against statutory income caps (≤ ₹2.5L/yr) for 100% tuition waivers and stipends.
  • Phone Number [Encrypted at rest]: Used strictly for optional SMS/WhatsApp roadmap delivery and session retrieval.
  • Age & Gender: Required to verify scheme age thresholds (18–50 yrs) and women-specific sub-schemes.
  • Education & Trade Skills: Evaluated against NSQF qualification pack entry prerequisites.
  • Delhi District / Location: Used to match nearest local accredited training centres (Govt ITIs / JSS / PMKK).

No biometric, financial bank, or extraneous tracking data is ever stored. Sensitive fields are encrypted at rest with Fernet. You may permanently purge your data anytime with the "Delete All My Data" button above.
